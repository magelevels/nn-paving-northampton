More room for everyday moments.

From a small seating area to paving that connects the whole garden, a patio changes how you use the outdoors. Consider where the sun falls, how you move from the house and how much room you need around your table or chairs.

Porcelain paving

A clean, contemporary look with considered joints and edges. Discuss colours, slab sizes and appropriate outdoor materials to suit the feel of your home.

Paths, curves and edges

Straight lines can create a calm, modern layout; curves can soften the transition into a lawn. NN Paving’s gallery shows paving wrapping around a newly finished garden.

Plan the practical details

Finished levels, door thresholds, drainage and access all need to work together. Include these details when describing your project so the conversation starts with the whole space.

Planning your project

How much will my driveway or patio cost?

Every space is different. Size, materials, access, existing surfaces, drainage and the amount of groundwork all affect the cost. Ask for a quotation for your property rather than relying on a generic price per square metre.

How long does the work take?

Timing depends on project size, preparation, materials and weather. Discuss an estimated schedule and arrangements for access when requesting your quotation.

What guarantees and payment terms are offered?

Ask Leon to confirm any applicable workmanship or product guarantees, payment schedule and exclusions in writing with your quotation. Terms are project-specific; no fixed guarantee or finance plan is advertised on this website.
