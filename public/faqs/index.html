Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
A clearer starting point.

A little planning makes the first conversation more useful. Here are some of the things to consider before starting your outdoor project.

How do I get a quotation?

Call Leon on 07999 749569 or use our quote planner to prepare an email. Include your postcode, the type of work and a few details about your space. Quotations are free and without obligation.

How much will my driveway or patio cost?

Every space is different. Size, materials, access, existing surfaces, drainage and the amount of groundwork all affect the cost. Ask for a quotation for your property rather than relying on a generic price per square metre.

Can I send photographs of my space?

Yes. Email photographs to NNPaving@gmail.com. A wide view, access to the area and any awkward levels are a useful starting point. You can add attachments after the quote planner opens your email app.

Do you cover my area?

NN Paving is based in Northampton. For nearby towns and villages, send your postcode so Leon can confirm availability and coverage for your project.

How long does the work take?

Timing depends on project size, preparation, materials and weather. Discuss an estimated schedule and arrangements for access when requesting your quotation.

What should I ask about drainage and permissions?

Discuss drainage, changes in levels, access and any permissions before work starts. Requirements depend on your property and the proposed design. Check the current guidance from your local planning authority where relevant.

What guarantees and payment terms are offered?

Ask Leon to confirm any applicable workmanship or product guarantees, payment schedule and exclusions in writing with your quotation. Terms are project-specific; no fixed guarantee or finance plan is advertised on this website.

How do I look after new paving?

Care depends on the surface and jointing system. Ask for the appropriate cleaning, maintenance and aftercare guidance for the materials used in your project.
